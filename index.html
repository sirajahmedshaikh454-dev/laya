A tihai is a phrase played three times so that the last bol of the third phrase lands exactly on sam. Give a phrase, say where it starts, and Laya finds every gap that works.

About the notation and the sound
  • x is sam (beat 1), 2, 3 are claps (tali), 0 is a wave (khali). In Rupak, sam is itself a khali beat. Thekas follow the common forms of each taal; schools differ in detail.
  • Tihai convention. Here the last bol of the third phrase falls on sam. Some teachers finish the third phrase one beat before sam and then play sam alone; shift your phrase by one pulse to hear that version.
  • The sound is synthesized. It is a sketch of tabla, good for keeping time and hearing a phrase, not a recording of a real drum.
